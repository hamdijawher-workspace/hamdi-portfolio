Profile / International

I make complex products easier to use.

I’m Jawher Hamdi. I design useful products and the systems behind them.

My work spans biometric self-service, enterprise software and payment experiences at TLScontact, FORVIA and Addinn Group. I connect research with clear flows, interfaces and reusable patterns.

An art-direction background informs my eye for detail. I work closely with product owners, engineers and designers from discovery through delivery.
