Profile / International

I make complex products easier to use.

I’m Jawher Hamdi, also known as Jay Hamdi—a Product Designer and UX Designer working across research, interaction design and design systems.

Across Addinn Group and FORVIA Faurecia, I have worked on payment services, enterprise data applications and reusable interface systems. I connect stakeholder needs with user journeys, turn complex requirements into understandable flows, and carry those decisions into prototypes and documentation.

My art-direction background adds a strong sense of hierarchy, composition and visual consistency. Product design is the centre of my practice: understanding what people need to do, reducing friction, and helping teams make informed decisions. I enjoy working closely with product owners, engineers and other designers from discovery through delivery.
