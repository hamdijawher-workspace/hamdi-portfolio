Work together

Make the next product decision clearer.

I’m open to product and UX design roles, as well as focused collaborations with teams improving complex digital experiences.

01 / Discovery

Understand the problem

Stakeholder conversations, experience audits and journey mapping to clarify the audience, constraints and priorities. The output is a focused problem statement and a practical direction for design.

02 / Product design

Shape the experience

Information architecture, user flows, wireframes and interactive prototypes. Review the key tasks, edge cases and interaction states before committing to detailed interface design.

03 / Design systems

Create a shared language

Foundations, components, reusable patterns and documentation that help designers and engineers build consistently. Begin with the patterns the product actually needs.

04 / Iteration

Learn and refine

Usability sessions, design reviews and handoff support. Agree on what needs validation, prioritise the findings and keep decisions connected to user needs.
